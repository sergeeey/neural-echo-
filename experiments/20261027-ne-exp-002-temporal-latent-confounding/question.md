# Question

- **Research question:** Under which synthetic structures does a predictive gain of a behavioural covariate over a wheel-free nuisance set survive, and which structures does the procedure fail to distinguish? (Stress test of identifiability, not an analysis of real signal.)
- **Question type (L0):** descriptive of the procedure's behaviour under known truth. No causal claim about the animal.
- **Out of scope:** any claim about the real fUS signal, the animal's physiology, or neural mechanism. No holdout. No real-data fitting.
