# Alternative hypotheses

H1 (target): wheel velocity adds held-out predictive information beyond eye and pupil in sub-ALD002 (Layer 1).

H2 (alternative): the predictive gain comes from a variable correlated with the wheel and not measured (for example arousal), so the gain is not wheel-specific. Under H2 the gain persists with a hidden confound and disappears when that confound is measured. NE-EXP-002 showed this pattern for the synthetic design.

H3 (artifact): the gain comes from the structure of the wheel series itself (exact-zero mass, episode structure, heteroskedasticity) interacting with the fold models, not from a relation to fUS. A synthetic null with the observed wheel structure but no outcome relation must produce no gain.

Procedure checks (synthetic; they test the procedure, not the real data, and do not discriminate H1 from H2 or H3 in the real recording):
- H2 vs H1: hidden-arousal scenario versus measured-arousal scenario (existing controls). Under H2 the measured-arousal scenario must give Δ ≈ 0.
- H3 vs H1: zero-effect synthetic outcome on the real wheel. Under H3 Δ > 0 without any injected effect.
