# Alternative hypotheses

| ID | Hypothesis | Prediction under this hypothesis | Prediction that would refute it |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Wheel velocity adds predictive information for the fUS signal beyond eye and pupil | Gain above the block-permutation null | Gain within the null |
| H2 | The signal is explained by eye and pupil (arousal-linked state) | Gain within the null | Gain above the null |
| H0 | The gain is body-motion artefact, or locomotion-related blood flow rather than the coupling of interest | Gain persists, and the lag profile is compatible with motion (see protocol) | Not separable with current data |

**Discriminating test:** none is available that separates H0 from H1 in this dataset. The session-mismatched test was removed because it does not separate them. Until a motion-independent reference is verified, H0 stays open, and any reading beyond predictive R² is blocked. The claim therefore cannot be stronger than "predictive gain above null, motion not excluded".
