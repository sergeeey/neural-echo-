# Claim

- **Claim (predictive, exploratory):** In sub-ALD002 development runs, adding signed wheel velocity to eye and pupil signals lowers held-out log loss for the whole-slice power-Doppler signal, compared with a block-permutation null, consistently across calendar dates.
- **Scope:** predictive only. This claim makes **no neural, neurovascular or intention claim.** Locomotion changes blood flow and body movement; the signal may reflect either. Motion is not excluded.
- **Evidence label:** HYPOTHESIS (H-001). Classified EXPLORATORY.
- **Prediction if true:** Δ = L_nuisance − L_full is positive and exceeds the 95th percentile of the block-permutation null, with Δ > 0 on most held-out dates.
- **Reportable outcomes (MDE and MCID not set, so no falsification language):**
  - "Δ above block-permutation null" (descriptive, exploratory);
  - "not detected above null" when Δ lies within the null;
  - "uninformative" when the design cannot separate the readings (e.g. timing mapping unresolved).
  "Falsified", "refuted", "no effect" are not used.
- **Language to avoid:** "decodes", "reads", "thought", "intention", "image", "neural", "neurovascular".
