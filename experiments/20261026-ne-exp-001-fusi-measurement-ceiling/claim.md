# Claims

Revised 2026-10-27 while the protocol is PLANNED. The earlier single claim is withdrawn: it mixed prediction with mechanism. Each statement below stands alone, has its own status, and is judged only on its own evidence.

## 1. Predictive association (the only claim under test)

- **Statement:** adding signed wheel velocity (lags 0 to 6 s) to eye movement and pupil size improves held-out prediction of whole-slice power-Doppler in the eight count-matched sub-ALD002 runs, by more than a block-permutation null, with Δ > 0 on at least 5 of 6 dates.
- **Status:** HYPOTHESIS, EXPLORATORY. Testable with this design.
- **Evidence now:** synthetic controls only (see synthetic_controls_preliminary.md). No real-signal result.
- **Reportable outcomes:** "above null", "not above null", or "uninformative". No "falsified" or "no effect" language, because MDE and MCID are not set.

## 2. Temporal specificity (the wheel leads the signal)

- **Statement:** the wheel-to-fUS relation has the lag direction and window assumed in the protocol.
- **Status:** NOT TESTABLE with this design. Synthetic delayed (6 s) and advanced (2.1 s) effects are both detected at high rates (65% and 80%). The data cannot select the direction, and the VolumeTiming convention and clock alignment are unverified.
- **Consequence:** no sentence in any report may state or imply the direction of time.

## 3. Wheel-specific contribution

- **Statement:** the predictive gain in statement 1 is due to wheel velocity itself, rather than to an unmeasured variable correlated with it.
- **Status:** NOT TESTABLE with this design. A hidden arousal correlated with the wheel (r ≈ 0.5) produces detection in about 93% of synthetic replicates.
- **Consequence:** statement 1 may not be described as a wheel effect.

## 4. Neural or physiological mechanism

- **Statement:** the signal reflects neural activity, neurovascular coupling, or any other specific mechanism.
- **Status:** NOT TESTABLE. Body motion, locomotion-related blood flow, and arousal are not excluded, and no motion-independent reference is verified.
- **Consequence:** no neural or mechanistic wording in any output.

## Language rule

Only statement 1 may be reported as a result, and only with the qualifiers above. Statements 2 to 4 may be stated only as "not established by this design".
