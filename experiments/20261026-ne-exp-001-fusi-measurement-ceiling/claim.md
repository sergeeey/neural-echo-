# Claim

- **Claim (predictive, exploratory):** In sub-ALD002 development runs, adding wheel velocity to eye and pupil signals increases held-out R² for the whole-slice power-Doppler signal, by an amount that exceeds the block-permutation null distribution.
- **Scope:** predictive only. This claim makes **no neural, neurovascular, or intention claim.** Locomotion changes blood flow and body movement; the signal may reflect either. Motion is not excluded.
- **Evidence label:** HYPOTHESIS (H-001). Classified EXPLORATORY.
- **Prediction if true:** the pooled gain exceeds the 95th percentile of the block-permutation null, and the gain is positive in most runs.
- **Reportable outcomes (MDE and MCID not set, so no falsification language):**
  - "gain above block-permutation null" (descriptive, exploratory);
  - "not detected above null" when the gain is within the null;
  - "uninformative" when the design cannot separate the two (e.g. no clean reference is available).
  "Falsified", "refuted", "no effect" are not used.
- **Success criterion:** as above; fixed before data are evaluated. No effect size is claimed.
- **Language to avoid:** "decodes", "reads", "thought", "intention", "image", "neural", "neurovascular".
