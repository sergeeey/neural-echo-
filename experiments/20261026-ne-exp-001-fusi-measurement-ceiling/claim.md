# Claim

- **Claim (exploratory, falsifiable):** In sub-ALD002, wheel velocity explains held-out whole-slice power-Doppler variance beyond eye and pupil signals: the held-out R² gain is positive, and the leave-one-run-out split gives a gain above zero in a majority of runs.
- **Evidence label:** HYPOTHESIS (H-001). Classified EXPLORATORY: no statistical claim at alpha 0.05 is made (see decision.md).
- **Prediction if true:** the held-out R² gain is positive in most runs and in pooled analysis.
- **Falsification criterion:** the pooled gain is zero or negative, or the gain is positive in fewer than half of the runs.
- **Success criterion:** as above; fixed before data are evaluated. No effect size is claimed.
- **Scope limit (blocking for any neural reading):** wheel movement moves the animal's body and head. Power-Doppler is sensitive to motion. No motion-independent reference signal has been identified in the data (see controls.md). Until one is verified, a positive result reads only as "fUS predicts wheel velocity beyond eye and pupil; motion not excluded".
- **Language to avoid:** "decodes", "reads", "thought", "intention", "image".
